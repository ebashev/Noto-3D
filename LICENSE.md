## Google Noto 3D artwork

The PNG and WebP images are derived from Google Noto 3D artwork. Copyright notices and modifications are listed in [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md). The MIT licenses above do not apply to Google's artwork.

Upstream licensing statements are inconsistent: the [repository README](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/blob/06121655d0e82f9cae6e7ba6feed4fa6fdbfc2a4/README.md) assigns Apache 2.0 to most image resources, while its [root LICENSE](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/blob/06121655d0e82f9cae6e7ba6feed4fa6fdbfc2a4/LICENSE) contains OFL 1.1. The [gallery FAQ](https://googlefonts.github.io/noto-emoji-files/) explicitly names CC BY 4.0 for animated emoji, without a separate explicit grant for static 3D PNGs.

The upstream OFL text and the referenced Apache 2.0 text are retained in `licenses/`. Their inclusion does not assert that they are interchangeable or grant additional rights to the artwork. The applicable license for redistribution of static 3D assets requires clarification from upstream.
